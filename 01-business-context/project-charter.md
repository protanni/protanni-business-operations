# PROTANNI Business Operations Launch Readiness Sprint — Project Charter

**Method:** PMI KICKOFF — Predictive  
**Project window:** 21 Sep–22 Nov 2026  
**Project lead / sponsor:** Natanni Fernandes  
**Status:** Active

## Business Need / Project Objectives

PROTANNI is a pre-launch digital productivity product that needs an integrated Business Operations system for launch decisions and scalable execution.

This project will:

- Build a 9-week operating foundation that converts product strategy into measurable KPIs, analytics, financial scenarios, GTM/RevOps processes, and governance.
- Create decision-ready performance management, forecasting, and resource-allocation logic.
- Define launch, lifecycle/CRM, and commercial operating processes with clear owners, metrics, and review cadences.
- Produce a reproducible, privacy-safe case study based on the real business without exposing production code or confidential data.

## Project Requirements

- Use PROTANNI's real pre-launch context, current product scope, and actual business constraints.
- Keep production code, credentials, user data, and confidential commercial information private.
- Use synthetic/anonymized data for public analytics and clearly label assumptions and modeled metrics.
- Execute five phases from 21 Sep to 22 Nov 2026 at approximately 10–12 hours/week; intensive weeks may reach ~14 hours.
- Apply phase exit criteria before advancing.
- Ensure every artifact supports a business question, decision, or operating process.
- Use a free-first learning/tooling approach; paid credentials require a defined readiness/value gate.

## Deliverables

1. **Data foundation:** business context, charter, KPI tree, data dictionary/model, synthetic dataset, and SQL analyses.
2. **BI & planning:** executive Power BI dashboard, driver-based forecast, budget/unit economics, operating model, and business case.
3. **GTM & RevOps:** ICP/positioning, GTM strategy, funnel, launch plan, CRM architecture, lifecycle, and pipeline design.
4. **Governance & automation:** project-management artifacts, two operational automations, applied AI/Python use cases, and decision memos.
5. **Operations excellence:** Lean/RCA improvement case, change/adoption plan, vendor scorecard, regional operating model, and final QBR/case study.
6. Public outputs are packaged in this repository; the production application remains private.

## Out of Scope

- Shipping or rebuilding production app features, backend, billing/paywall, store release, or engineering remediation.
- Publishing private production code, secrets, real user-level data, or confidential business information.
- Hiring a formal team or claiming people-management, enterprise P&L, Salesforce-admin, or market-expansion experience not actually held.
- Executing international expansion or presenting modeled/pre-launch metrics as actual traction or performance.

## High-Level Risks

- Solo-founder capacity and context switching may cause schedule slippage while product work continues in parallel.
- Pre-launch status means limited real acquisition, revenue, and retention data; modeled outputs can create false precision if not labeled.
- Product or technical scope may change during the 9 weeks, making operating artifacts stale.
- Public portfolio work could expose private code, sensitive data, or commercial assumptions if privacy governance fails.
- Scope creep across BI, finance, GTM, RevOps, automation, and operations excellence could reduce depth and quality.
- Tool/course access or credential costs may delay optional outputs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All five phase exit criteria are met by 22 Nov 2026, and the final QBR confirms completion or explicitly records carry-over items.
- The public repository explains the business problem, scope, methods, and outputs in under two minutes.
- KPI/data governance are documented; dashboard, forecast, and financial model support decisions and scenario analysis.
- GTM/RevOps operating model links ICP → funnel → lifecycle → metrics → owners/actions.
- Governance, risks, dependencies, decisions, and milestones are traceable; automation and operations-excellence artifacts are demonstrable.
- No private code, secrets, or sensitive real data are published; synthetic/assumed metrics are clearly labeled and reproducible.

## Assumptions

- PROTANNI remains pre-launch during most or all of the sprint; this project prepares operating readiness rather than claiming live performance.
- Current product scope is stable enough to model business operations; material changes will be versioned in the artifacts.
- Natanni can dedicate approximately 10–12 hours/week and acts as sponsor, PM, and BizOps owner.
- Synthetic/anonymized data can represent realistic funnel/revenue/retention mechanics until sufficient real data exists.
- Required free tools remain available; PL-300 spend occurs only after the readiness gate.
- Initial role ownership may map to the founder because PROTANNI does not yet have a formal operating team.

## Constraints

- Fixed timeline: 21 Sep–22 Nov 2026; limited weekly capacity and no dedicated operating team.
- Limited cash: planned paid education is USD 99 for PL-300 after gate; avoid unnecessary software/certification spend.
- Production application code is private and cannot be published in the case study.
- No fabrication of traction, revenue, retention, market performance, or team ownership.
- Project work must not destabilize the private product; production engineering changes require a separate approval/workflow.
- Public outputs must be privacy-safe, concise, recruiter-readable, and maintainable as the product evolves.
