# PROTANNI — Business Context & Scope

**Stage:** Pre-launch product in active development  
**Business model:** Subscription-based B2C SaaS with a planned free trial  
**Initial market scope:** Brazil + international / English-speaking  
**Business Operations initiative:** 21 Sep–22 Nov 2026

## 1. Company / Product Overview

**PROTANNI is a calm personal operating system that brings tasks, routines, daily execution and reflection into one place.**

The product is designed to help individual consumers reduce the friction created by fragmented productivity tools, mental overload, and overcomplicated planning systems. Instead of optimizing for maximum output, PROTANNI is built around a calmer operating model: clarify what matters, act on it today, maintain routines, and understand progress over time.

PROTANNI is currently a **pre-launch product in active development**. Product development is real and ongoing, while the surrounding Business Operations system is being formalized before launch and scale.

## 2. Customer Problem

The current problem definition is centered on people who:

- manage tasks, routines, priorities, and reflections across disconnected tools or mental systems;
- spend too much effort organizing before they can execute;
- struggle to identify what matters most today;
- experience inconsistency between planning, action, and review;
- want structure without turning productivity into pressure.

The business context therefore treats productivity friction as an operating-system problem rather than only a task-management problem.

## 3. Target Customer

The current target audience is:

> **Individual consumers who want a simpler, calmer way to organize tasks, routines and daily priorities.**

This is intentionally broad at this stage. Detailed ICP, segmentation, personas, and prioritization criteria will be developed later in the GTM phase rather than assumed prematurely in the Business Context phase.

## 4. Value Proposition

PROTANNI's current value proposition is to create one calm control layer for personal execution.

The product aims to help users:

- centralize tasks and routines;
- translate planning into clear daily action;
- reduce cognitive load and decision friction;
- observe progress and consistency without perfection-based pressure;
- use AI selectively to reduce friction in reflection and daily direction.

The product is not positioned as an AI chatbot or as a pure task manager. AI is an enabling capability within the broader personal operating system.

## 5. Core Product Capabilities

The current core is:

### Today
The daily execution layer: what requires attention now and what should guide the user's day.

### Tasks
The structured work layer for obligations, actions, and executable work.

### Routines
The recurring-behavior layer used to support consistency over time.

### Progress
The feedback layer used to help users understand trends, rhythm, and progress rather than only completion counts.

### AI
AI-assisted experiences supporting:
- mood;
- daily focus;
- reflection.

These capabilities represent the current business-level product scope. Detailed technical implementation remains outside this public Business Operations case.

## 6. Business Model

PROTANNI is planned as a:

> **Subscription-based B2C SaaS with a planned free trial.**

At this stage, the Business Context does not lock a final public price, trial duration, CAC target, LTV target, or packaging structure. Those decisions require evidence from financial modeling, GTM planning, market validation, and later operating analysis.

## 7. Market Scope

The initial market scope is:

> **Brazil + international / English-speaking.**

The product is being considered for both the Brazilian market and international English-speaking users. This describes intended launch scope, not existing market traction.

No revenue, acquisition, retention, or geographic-performance claim should be interpreted as current operating performance unless later supported by real production data.

## 8. Current Operating Context

PROTANNI is founder-led and pre-launch.

The product has progressed far enough that the next operating challenge is no longer only product construction. The company also needs a repeatable system for deciding:

- what success means;
- which metrics matter;
- how performance should be reviewed;
- how assumptions become measurable hypotheses;
- how resources and financial scenarios should be evaluated;
- how acquisition, activation, engagement, conversion, revenue, and retention should connect;
- how launch initiatives, risks, dependencies, and decisions should be governed.

This creates the need for a Business Operations layer around the existing product.

## 9. Current Business Challenge

The central business challenge for this sprint is:

> **Determine how PROTANNI should be measured, operated and prepared for launch using a repeatable Business Operations system.**

The gap is not the absence of a product. The gap is the absence of a sufficiently formalized operating system around the product.

The sprint therefore converts the existing product context into measurable, decision-ready business infrastructure.

## 10. Business Operations Scope

### In Scope

The Business Operations initiative includes:

- business context and project governance;
- KPI and performance-management framework;
- data definitions, privacy rules, and analytical model;
- SQL-based business analysis;
- executive BI and decision-support views;
- forecasting and scenario planning;
- budgeting, unit economics, and business-case logic;
- GTM planning and funnel definition;
- CRM / lifecycle / pipeline operating design;
- operating cadences and ownership logic;
- automation and AI-enabled operational workflows;
- process improvement and root-cause analysis;
- change/adoption planning;
- vendor/partner evaluation;
- regional operating-model design;
- final QBR and case-study packaging.

### Out of Scope

This initiative does **not** include:

- production feature development;
- backend or infrastructure refactoring;
- general engineering remediation;
- billing/paywall implementation;
- app-store or production-release execution;
- publication of private source code;
- publication of private user data, secrets, credentials, or sensitive business information;
- presentation of modeled data as real traction;
- claims of people-management, enterprise P&L ownership, or operating scale that PROTANNI does not currently have.

If Business Operations work identifies a product or engineering requirement, it may create a recommendation, requirement, risk, or decision record. Implementation remains a separate product/engineering workstream.

## 11. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in this initiative |
|---|---|
| Founder / Sponsor / Business Operations Owner | Sets scope and priorities, approves decisions, and owns delivery. |
| PROTANNI Product Workstream | Provides the real product context, constraints, and product dependencies that Business Operations artifacts must reflect. |
| Prospective / Beta Users | Provide future evidence for customer needs, value proposition, usability, and behavioral assumptions. |
| Technology / SaaS Providers | Create cost, reliability, privacy, integration, and scalability dependencies. |
| Public Case-Study Reviewers | Consume portfolio-safe evidence without access to production code or sensitive information. |

## 12. Decision Scope

This project is intended to build the evidence and operating structure required to answer, progressively:

1. How should PROTANNI define and measure product and business performance?
2. Which KPIs require targets, thresholds, owners, and review cadences?
3. Which data is required to support acquisition, activation, engagement, conversion, revenue, and retention decisions?
4. How should launch and growth scenarios be forecast and evaluated financially?
5. How should the first GTM operating model connect audience, channels, funnel, lifecycle, and measurement?
6. Which operational processes should be standardized, automated, monitored, or improved?
7. How should business decisions, dependencies, risks, and corrective actions be governed?

These questions are resolved through later sprint deliverables; they are not pre-answered in this document.

## 13. Assumptions

- PROTANNI remains pre-launch while the Business Operations foundation is built.
- The product remains in active development, so business artifacts may require controlled updates when product scope changes.
- The company remains founder-led during the current sprint.
- Initial analysis may use synthetic or anonymized data until sufficient real operating data exists.
- Brazil and international / English-speaking users remain the intended launch scope unless later evidence changes that direction.
- Pricing, trial mechanics, ICP detail, and channel mix may be refined during later financial and GTM work.

## 14. Constraints

- Fixed sprint window: 21 Sep–22 Nov 2026.
- Limited founder capacity and no dedicated operating team.
- Production source code remains private.
- Public outputs must be portfolio-safe and maintainable.
- Pre-launch status limits the amount of real behavioral and commercial data available.
- Modeled or synthetic information must always be labeled as such.
- Product-development work and Business Operations work must remain distinct enough to avoid scope creep.

## 15. Definition of Done for the Business Context

This Business Context is considered complete when an external reader can answer, without consulting another document:

- What is PROTANNI?
- Who is it currently intended for?
- What problem does it address?
- What stage is the product in?
- What is the planned business model?
- Which markets are currently in scope?
- What are the core product capabilities?
- Why does the company need a Business Operations initiative now?
- What is inside and outside the scope of the initiative?
- Which decisions should the 9-week sprint enable?

With those questions answerable, the Business Context becomes the source document for the KPI framework, data model, financial model, GTM plan, operating model, and final case study.
